Beef Wet Fry

A hearty and flavorful beef stir fry cooked with tomatoes, onions, and spices, perfect for a quick weeknight dinner.

Ingredients

  • Beef
  • Tomatoes
  • Onions
  • Garlic
  • Cooking oil

Instructions

  1. Cook the beef.
  2. Add the onions and garlic.
  3. Add the tomatoes and vegetables.
  4. Simmer and serve.
