Hello Gotham City!

Over in the Diamond District, things got loud around midnight. Guests at the Monarch Theatre’s late gala had their evening cut short when the power grid suddenly spiked. Every digital billboard in a four-block radius briefly flashed a bright green question mark before going completely dark. Police say nothing was actually stolen from the gala, which somehow makes everyone in City Hall even more nervous.

On the bright side, the Gotham Knights pulled off an overtime win against Metropolis last night, so at least one thing in this city went right.

That’s your morning rundown, Gotham. Keep your umbrellas handy, watch your step near the docks, and stay safe out there. I’m Vicki Vale—we’ll be back tomorrow morning if the city lets us.
