Beef wet fry recipe

This is a simple recipe for the traditional east african style beef wet fry.

Ingredients

  • 500g beef, cut into small pieces
  • 2 onions, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 inch ginger, minced
  • 1-2 green chilies, chopped
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Fresh coriander leaves for garnish

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a pan and sauté onions until golden brown.
  2. Add garlic, ginger, and green chilies, and sauté for a minute.
  3. Add beef pieces and cook until browned.
  4. Add tomatoes, turmeric powder, cumin powder, coriander powder, and salt. Mix well.
  5. Cover and cook on low heat until the beef is tender and the oil separates.
  6. Garnish with fresh coriander leaves and serve hot.
